# Relation guidelines

<!-- Fill in: what to mark, the borderline cases and how to decide them, with examples

Each question marks two entities in a passage. Choose the relation the passage states
between them, or NONE when it states none. Where the task lets you choose more than one
(a relation group whose labels aren't exclusive), choose every relation the passage
states for the pair, and NONE only on its own.
