# Settling disagreements

Each text here is one where an annotator and the classifier gave different answers,
or an annotator chose UNCLEAR. Every answer is shown as a version of the text,
credited to where it came from: `LLM` for the classifier, and the annotation
dataset for each annotator. Keep the version that's right, or pick another label.
Your answer becomes the reference label.

## How to answer

- **Pick the label the definitions call for**, whichever of the two answers it
  matches, or neither. Don't split the difference, and don't favour either source.
- **Choose UNCLEAR only if the definitions genuinely don't settle it.** Each of these
  is a definition decision for the owner of the classifier; note what it would take
  to decide it.
- **Note the cause when it's clear**: the classifier misread the text, the annotator
  slipped, the definitions are ambiguous, or the text itself is broken. The causes,
  more than the counts, say what to fix: the prompt, the guidelines, or the data.
