# Reviewing uncertain records

The classifier decides most records on its own. You see only the ones it wasn't
confident about, so expect the hard cases: short or ambiguous texts, jargon, and
texts that sit between two labels.

## How to answer

- **Pick the label the definitions call for.** The definitions shown with each text,
  together with the rules below, are the standard; when they settle a case, follow
  them even if you'd personally decide otherwise.
- **If no label fits or you can't decide**, skip the text with the ignore button and
  note why. Recurring skips are a sign the label set or definitions need a change.
